Plug-in architecture

From upload to evidence

Images run once; video is sampled frame-by-frame, re-annotated and re-encoded as MP4 so CPU Spaces stay responsive.

1

Upload

Drop an image or a video clip into the all-models tab or a single model.

2

Detect

Loaded models run inference — YOLO draws boxes, the CNN predicts a class.

3

Annotate

Boxes, labels and confidence are burned onto the frame; video re-encodes to MP4.

4

Export

Download a CSV — one row per detection: model, class, confidence and box.
